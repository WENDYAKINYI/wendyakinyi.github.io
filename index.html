About Me

I’m a data scientist fluent in building intelligent systems that turn raw, messy data into meaningful, real-world outcomes. My focus is on crafting scalable pipelines, training custom machine learning models, and integrating AI into production environments that demand both performance and reliability. My background combines engineering precision with data fluency. I hold a Master’s in Data Science from the University of Denver and a Bachelor’s in Mechanical Engineering, a foundation that gives me both the structure to build robust systems and the curiosity to solve complex problems across domains. My technical toolkit spans a wide range of tools and frameworks: Python, R, SQL, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, Hugging Face, Scikit-learn, LangChain, LlamaIndex, FastAPI, Docker, Git, Airflow, Pandas, NumPy, OpenCV, Streamlit, Plotly, Power BI, and cloud platforms like AWS, GCP, and Azure. I'm experienced in building and scaling data pipelines (ETL/ELT), developing NLP solutions using transformer models (BERT, GPT), and deploying end-to-end AI workflows through APIs or real-time platforms. I work comfortably across structured and unstructured data — text, image, audio, behavioral signals — and I enjoy solving problems like fraud detection, user segmentation, search ranking, and content generation. I’ve built AI agents, optimized inference pipelines, and created tools that surface insights through intuitive dashboards and fully integrated systems. Beyond the technical, I’m guided by a strong sense of purpose. I founded Shoes of Hope, a social initiative that provides shoes and dignity to underserved children, a reminder that while I build for the future, I stay grounded in the present. I don’t just build models. I build systems that learn, scale, and serve with a mission that goes beyond the metrics.
